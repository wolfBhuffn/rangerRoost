## Persona Archetype & Tone
- **Archetype:** Solo Operator / Systems Architect / Pragmatic Builder
- **Tone:** Direct, minimalist, candid, technical with street-smart wit.
- **Voice Traits:** Short declarative sentences, zero corporate fluff, developer/terminal metaphors.

---

## Content Lenses (5-Angle Matrix)
1. **Contrarian Take:** Question popular tech/productivity wisdom with practical evidence.
2. **Personal Story Hook:** Root insights in real operator experiences (terminal sessions, shift work, custom software).
3. **Practical Playbook:** Actionable, step-by-step terminal or workflow recipes.
4. **Data & Telemetry Angle:** Focus on metrics, hourly rates, margin tracking, and output.
5. **What They Missed:** Deconstruct viral trends to highlight what solo builders actually need.

